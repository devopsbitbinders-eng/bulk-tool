Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2025  ·  Effective immediately

Welcome to BulkPulse, a WhatsApp bulk messaging platform operated by Bitbinders. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our platform. By using BulkPulse, you agree to the terms described below.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information to provide and improve our service:

  • Account Information: Username and hashed password when you register.
  • WhatsApp Credentials: Access tokens and phone number IDs provided through Meta's Embedded Login, stored securely and encrypted.
  • Campaign Data: Phone number lists, message templates, and campaign results that you upload or create.
  • Chat History: Messages sent and received through your linked WhatsApp Business Account.
  • Usage Data: IP address, browser type, and usage patterns to improve platform stability.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used exclusively to:

  • Authenticate your account and maintain session security.
  • Send WhatsApp messages on your behalf using your linked credentials.
  • Store and display your campaign history and analytics.
  • Provide customer support and resolve technical issues.
  • Improve platform features and reliability.

We do not sell, share, or disclose your data to third parties for marketing purposes.

3. WhatsApp & Meta Data

BulkPulse integrates with the WhatsApp Business API provided by Meta. By linking your WhatsApp Business Account, you authorize BulkPulse to send messages on your behalf. Your WhatsApp access tokens are stored securely and used only to interact with the Meta Graph API.

All messaging activity must comply with WhatsApp Business Policy and Meta Platform Terms.

4. Data Isolation & Security

All user data is strictly isolated. Each account can only access its own campaigns, templates, chat history, and credentials. We implement industry-standard security measures including:

  • Password hashing using SHA-256 with salt.
  • Secure session tokens with expiry.
  • HTTPS encryption for all data in transit.
  • Parameterized database queries to prevent SQL injection.
  • Per-user data isolation on all API endpoints.

5. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us. Campaign logs and message records may be retained for up to 90 days after deletion for legal compliance.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your data.
  • Withdraw consent for data processing.
  • File a complaint with your local data protection authority.

7. Cookies

We use a single session cookie (session_token) to keep you logged into the dashboard. This cookie is essential for platform functionality and does not track you across other websites. No third-party analytics cookies are used.

8. Third-Party Services

BulkPulse may integrate with the following third-party services:

  • Meta / WhatsApp Business API – for message delivery.
  • Google Sheets API – optional, for importing recipient lists.
  • Email (SMTP) – optional, for campaign report delivery.

Each of these services has their own privacy policies, which we encourage you to review.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any significant changes by updating the date at the top of this page. Continued use of BulkPulse after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
