01

Overview

Bit Binders ("we", "our", or "us") operates the WhatsApp Campaign Tool — a platform that enables businesses to send bulk WhatsApp messages to their opted-in contacts using the WhatsApp Business API via Meta's platform.

This policy governs the Bit Binders application (App ID: 916270141105838) and its integration with the WhatsApp Business API.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how we protect it. By using our tool, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

⚠️ We are committed to complying with Meta's Platform Policies, WhatsApp Business Policy, and all applicable data protection laws including GDPR and India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

02

Data We Collect

We collect the following categories of information when you use our tool:

Account & Authentication Data
  • Facebook account name and profile ID (via Facebook Login)
  • Connected WhatsApp Business Account (WABA) details
  • Phone number ID linked to your WhatsApp Business Account
  • OAuth access tokens provided by Meta
Campaign & Usage Data
  • CSV / XLSX files uploaded containing recipient phone numbers
  • Message templates selected or created by you
  • Campaign name, launch date/time, and status
  • Message delivery status per recipient (Sent / Failed)
  • Report email address (if provided)
Technical Data
  • IP address and browser/device information
  • Log data (errors, API call timestamps)
  • Session identifiers
03

How We Use Your Data

We use collected data only for the purposes listed below:

  • To authenticate your identity via Facebook Login and connect your WhatsApp Business Account
  • To process uploaded recipient lists and send WhatsApp messages on your behalf
  • To track and record the delivery status of each message in the campaign
  • To generate and deliver campaign reports to your specified email
  • To maintain campaign history accessible within your dashboard
  • To maintain security, prevent abuse, and ensure platform stability
  • To comply with Meta's Platform Policies and legal obligations

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or sell it to any third party.

04

Facebook Login & WhatsApp Integration

Our tool uses Facebook Login to authenticate users and connect to their WhatsApp Business Account through Meta's official API. By logging in, you authorize us to:

  • Access your Facebook profile name and user ID
  • Access your connected WhatsApp Business Account and phone numbers
  • Send messages on your behalf using your approved WhatsApp templates
  • Read message delivery status from the WhatsApp Business API

🔒 We only request the minimum permissions required. We do not access your personal Facebook posts, friends list, messages, or any data unrelated to the WhatsApp Business API.

You can revoke our app's access at any time from your Facebook App Settings. Upon revocation, we will immediately stop accessing your WhatsApp account.

05

Recipient Data (Uploaded Contact Lists)

When you upload a CSV or XLSX file with recipient phone numbers, you represent and warrant that:

  • All recipients have explicitly opted in to receive WhatsApp messages from you
  • You have obtained proper consent in compliance with applicable laws
  • You have informed recipients of the nature of messages they will receive
  • You will honor opt-out requests from recipients promptly

Uploaded recipient data is used solely to dispatch the campaign messages you initiate. We do not store recipient phone numbers beyond what is necessary to complete the campaign and generate your delivery report.

Recipient files are processed in-memory and associated records are retained only for the duration needed to display your campaign history. You may clear your history at any time using the "Clear All" option in the dashboard.

06

Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information. We share data only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Meta / WhatsApp: Message content and recipient numbers are transmitted to Meta's WhatsApp Business API to deliver your messages. This is governed by Meta's own Privacy Policy.
  • Email delivery: If you provide a report email, we use an email service provider to send you your campaign report.
  • Legal requirements: We may disclose data if required by law, court order, or governmental authority.

No other third parties receive your data.

🇮🇳 India Data Localization: In compliance with Indian data regulations, all recipient contact lists uploaded to Bit Binders are processed on secure servers and are not shared with any third party other than Meta for the sole purpose of message delivery.

07

Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data:

  • All data transmitted between your browser and our servers is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS
  • OAuth tokens from Meta are stored securely and never exposed to third parties
  • Access to backend systems is restricted to authorized personnel only
  • Uploaded files are processed securely and not stored publicly

While we take all reasonable steps to protect your data, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We encourage you to protect your Facebook account with a strong password and two-factor authentication.

08

Data Retention

We retain your data only as long as necessary:

  • Account data: Retained while your account is active or as required by law
  • Campaign history: Retained until you manually clear it from your dashboard
  • Uploaded recipient files: Processed for campaign delivery and not retained permanently after processing
  • Log data: Retained for up to 90 days for security and debugging purposes

Upon account deletion or access revocation, we will delete your personal data within 30 days, except where retention is required by law.

08.1

How to Request Data Deletion

🗑️ Data Deletion Instructions: Users may request the permanent deletion of their data (including OAuth tokens and uploaded contact lists) by emailing info@bitbinders.in. We will acknowledge the request within 24 hours and complete the deletion from our production servers within 10 business days.

Your deletion request must include your registered email address or Facebook account name so we can locate and remove your data accurately. Once deleted, this action cannot be undone.

  • OAuth tokens are revoked and removed from our systems immediately
  • Uploaded contact lists and campaign history are permanently deleted
  • Log data associated with your account is purged within 10 business days
  • A confirmation email will be sent once deletion is complete
